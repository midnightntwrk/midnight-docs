# Ledger

The ledger in Midnight is a public record of contract states and token states, consisting of Zswap’s cryptographic commitments and a mapping of contract addresses to their respective states.

[Link to related documentation](../../concepts/how-midnight-works/semantics)

To download the component, click the appropriate link under **Artifacts**.

## Component versions by release

A Ledger release includes the on-chain runtime, ZKIR (the zero-knowledge intermediate representation), and the proof server. Each has its own version number. To choose versions for a DApp, see the [compatibility matrix](./support-matrix).

Mainnet runs ledger 9 after the ledger 9 network upgrade. Preview was reset as a new ledger 9 chain on 6 November 2026. Preprod runs ledger 8.1.3 until a later upgrade. To prepare for the change, see the [ledger 9 upgrade page](./ledger-9).

The node embeds ledger 9.1.0.1. The packages that DApp and wallet builders use carry ledger 9.1.0.0.
